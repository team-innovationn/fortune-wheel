User Story Document: Raffle Draw Platform Role: Product Manager
Feature: Automated Raffle Draw Platform for Super Rewards Promo Epic 1 – Monthly Draw Categories
User Story 1 – Individual: New-to-Bank & Reactivation
As a product manager
I want the system to randomly select 10 winners per division (4 divisions total) from qualified individual customers
So that winners can be fairly chosen and displayed.
Acceptance Criteria:
● Customer details uploaded (Name, Account No, Branch, Region, Division, Phone, Email).
● Platform must draw 10 winners per division (4 draws).
● Display winner’s Name, Branch, masked Phone No.
● Ability to download full details of winners as uploaded.
User Story 2 – Individual: Bonus Reward
(Same as above, with identical logic for 10 winners per division × 4 divisions.)
User Story 3 – Individual: Super Reward
As a product manager
I want the platform to randomly select 1 winner per division from qualified individual customers
So that divisional winners are fairly displayed.
Acceptance Criteria:
● Same as User Story 1, but 1 winner per division × 4 divisions.
User Story 4 – Individual: Youth Category
As a product manager
I want the platform to randomly select 10 winners bank-wide from qualified youth customers So that winners are fairly displayed across the bank.
Acceptance Criteria:
● Customer details uploaded (Name, Account No, Branch, Region, Division, Phone, Email).
● Platform selects 10 winners across the bank (not divisional).
 ● Display winner’s Name, Branch, masked Phone No.
● Downloadable full details.

● Same criteria as for individuals.

User Story 7 – Business: Business Expansion

Non-Functional Requirements (NFRs):
● Random selection algorithms must ensure fairness and transparency.
● All draws must be auditable (logs of input file, winners, timestamp).
● Winner display must show masked phone numbers but allow full download
(CSV/Excel).
● Platform must support bulk data upload in Excel/CSV.
● Role-based access control (Admin to upload, Auditor to view logs).
● Platform should generate draw report for each event.
